# Acceptance Criteria

1. **[False positives]** Out of 10 passages I wrote myself (including at least 3 written in a plain, non-native style, like the second-language student from Milestone 1), at most 1 is labeled high-confidence AI, and none of the non-native-style passages are.

   *Why:* Wrongly flagging a real student is the worst thing this system can do, so 1 in 10 is the most I'll accept overall. The non-native passages get zero tolerance because that's exactly the person Milestone 1 showed getting hurt.

2. **[Score gap]** On 10 obviously AI-generated passages and 10 obviously human-written passages, the average combined confidence score is at least 0.30 higher (on a 0–1 scale) for the AI set, and at least 8 of the 10 AI passages score higher than the median score of the human set. The AI passages are generated by ChatGPT with no editing. The human passages are my own past essays, written before AI tools were available to me.

   *Why:* If the system can't clearly separate the easy cases, it can't be trusted on the hard ones. A small gap like 0.1 could just be noise, so 0.30 makes sure the difference is real and easy to see.

3. **[Label coverage]** I can find at least 2 different inputs for each of the 3 labels (6 inputs total) that produce that label, and I record each input and its output.

   *Why:* If one label never shows up, the thresholds are broken or the label is useless. Needing 2 inputs per label instead of 1 shows it isn't a one-off fluke.

4. **[Appeals]** In 10 out of 10 appeals, the submission's status changes to under_review and the log has a matching entry with the submission ID and a timestamp.

   *Why:* An appeal is how a wrongly flagged student gets a fair hearing. If even one appeal is silently dropped or goes unrecorded, the process can't be trusted, so anything less than 100% fails.

5. **[Bad input]** For each of these 4 cases (missing text field, empty string, whitespace-only text, and text over the length limit of 5000 characters), the system returns a 400 with a non-empty `message` field explaining the problem, never a 500, and writes nothing to the log. All 4 must pass.

   *Why:* Bad input is going to happen in real use. A crash or a junk log entry could mess up the appeal records or the results, so every case has to be handled cleanly.
